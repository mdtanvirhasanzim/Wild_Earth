The story

About the work.

Hello, I'm Tanvir.

I make photographs for people who still stop to watch the clouds.

My relationship with photography started with long walks and a simple curiosity: what changes when you stay in one place for another ten minutes?

That question eventually became a practice. I now work across landscapes, wildlife and intimate details of the natural world, creating visual stories for editorial, conservation and private collections.

The photographs are deliberately quiet. I want the viewer to feel weather, distance, texture and time — not just see a beautiful scene.

Journey

A decade of looking.

The milestones are useful, but the real journey happened between them: early mornings, missed shots, unexpected storms and thousands of patient observations.

2018

The beginning

Started documenting nearby forests and landscapes with a borrowed camera and a notebook full of locations.

2020

First major project

Completed a year-long visual study of changing seasons, rivers and rural landscapes.

2022

Beyond familiar ground

Expanded field work into mountain regions and international wildlife projects.

2024

First published collection

Released a curated print collection exploring solitude, distance and natural light.

2026

Current work

Building long-form projects around conservation, habitat and the human connection to wild places.

Equipment

Keep it simple.

I use my simple phone for most of my shots. I changed my phone but not my photography style . All Photos are taken by my Phone.

Subjects

Wild, quiet, changing.

Mountains, forests, birds, mammals, water, weather and the small patterns that appear when light moves across a landscape.

Approach

Patience first.

Observe before photographing. Leave places as found. Work with natural light whenever possible and prioritize low-impact field practice.
